› how do i tile three windows across my 4k screen in hyprland?

llama3.2 · local · no telemetry · your machine only

Hyprland tiles automatically. From an empty workspace:

  1. Open your first window (Super+Return for foot).
  2. Open the second — it splits the current pane in half.
  3. Open the third — it splits the focused pane again.

Use Super+J to toggle whether the split is horizontal
or vertical. Move focus with Super+arrows. Swap tiles
with Super+Shift+arrows.

If you want an exact 3-column layout on 4K, pin one window to full
height with Super+Alt+F then arrange the others in
the remaining space.
