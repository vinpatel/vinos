vinOS ships an opinionated default. Everything in this section is how
to change that default without giving up the parts you like.

- **[Keybindings](/docs/customization/keybindings/)** — the shipped
  Hyprland chord map, and how to add your own.
- **[Waybar](/docs/customization/waybar/)** — top bar layout,
  modules, and per-theme styling.
- **[Walker menu](/docs/customization/walker-menu/)** — app launcher,
  emoji picker, custom entries.
- **[First-boot customization](/docs/customization/first-boot-customization/)**
  — what `vinos-first-run` does, opting out of routines and bundles.
